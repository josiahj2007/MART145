Range Safety

Staying Safe

Safety is an important part of shooting. Following the rules of the range and handling firearms responsibly helps keep everyone safe.

Basic Safety Rules

  • Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
  • Always keep the firearm pointed in a safe direction.
  • Keep your finger off the trigger until ready to shoot.
  • Be sure of your target and what is behind it.
  • Wear eye and hearing protection.

More Information

Visit the NSSF firearm safety website to learn more about firearm safety.
